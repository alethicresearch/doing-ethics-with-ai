Review method

How to review without reopening everything.

The point of historical fidelity is controlled recovery, not endless comparison.

1 · Candidate first

Read the author-facing candidate as a paper. Mark only passages where something actually feels wrong, thin, generic, or displaced.

2 · Compare locally

Check CURRENT and then the named historical anchor only for that passage or conceptual issue. Do not reread every old version by default.

3 · Preserve the best layer

Keep current conceptual precision while restoring genuine lost voice, examples, argumentative movement, figures, or intellectual history.

4 · Make a decision

Each review ends in promote, targeted repair, or hold. Avoid producing another candidate that merely postpones the decision.
