Worked method

SACRE makes reflective equilibrium executable.

Structurally Analyzed Collective Reflective Equilibrium

SACRE maps cross-source normative convergence among Public, Expert, and Framework Policy candidates, ranks the resulting coherence field, and permits directed revision followed by renewed equilibration.

Step 5 is provisional. Step 6 revises selected positions and returns the updated configuration to the same evaluative procedure.

Evidence program

Validation asks different questions.

P2 · Computational

Does it behave reliably?

Repeatability, robustness, model/provider and representation effects, aggregation sensitivity, ranking stability, re-iteration, and alternative QCCS/convergence specifications including conv⁺, conv⁻, and conv±.

P3 · Human

How does it compare with people?

Human–human reliability, human–model correspondence, expert/lay variation, disagreement localization, and downstream ranking effects.

P4+ · Use

What happens in practice?

Feasibility, reliance, deliberation, documentation, governance, error recovery, user experience, and practical outcomes.

Domain realization

Computational bioethics.

Bioethics combines philosophical methods, empirical evidence, professional judgment, institutional procedures, and consequential decisions. It therefore provides a natural setting in which to build and test engineered normative methods.
